Sustainability

Plastic reduction you can measure

Our stance is simple: every environmental claim we make sits next to the test or study that supports it.

Our stance and approach

Practical, measured, specific

We reduce the fossil plastic in everyday products by replacing part of it with a mineral that would otherwise be waste.

We do not claim products are "zero impact" or "100% sustainable". We publish the baseline, the percentage by mass and the tested formulation for each grade, and we keep LCA results tied to their study boundary and date.
