Our process

Evaluation, pilot, testing

Every new account follows the same path from first sample to production.

Evaluation

We review your resin, process, part and targets, and recommend a grade.

Pilot

A trial run on your line with our technical team on hand.

Testing

Mechanical, processing and reduction tests against your baseline.

Production

Scale-up with supply terms and documentation.
