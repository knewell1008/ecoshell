What is Ecoshell

Bio calcium from the world's eggshells

CaCO3

Calcium carbonate, about 95% of eggshell by massVerify

Ecoshell develops eggshell-derived materials for plastic reduction and supports customers through masterbatches, finished products and manufacturing services.

We clean, mill and treat eggshell into a fine bio-calcium powder, then compound it into masterbatches that replace part of the polymer in a plastic product.
